# Filled Company Review Preferences Example

This is a synthetic example. It should not be used as real internal policy.

## Basic positioning

- Review role: seller-side
- Primary audience: business team and legal team
- Output detail level: standard
- Default risk posture: balanced

## Payment preferences

- Preferred payment method: bank transfer
- Payment methods requiring finance confirmation: commercial acceptance bills and long credit periods
- Minimum advance payment expectation: to be defined by the company
- Maximum acceptable post-acceptance payment exposure: to be confirmed by finance
- Late-payment responsibility requirement: interest or liquidated damages should be stated

## Liability preferences

- Preferred liability cap: to be defined by the company
- Indirect loss position: exclude or cap where appropriate
- Clauses requiring legal escalation: unlimited liability, broad indemnity, unclear dispute forum
