# Synthetic EPC Contract - Claim Time-Bar Risk

This is a synthetic fixture for testing. It does not describe a real project.

## Claims

The contractor shall submit any claim within 3 days after the event occurs. Failure to submit within the period means the contractor permanently waives the claim. The owner is not subject to the same period.

## Risk pattern

The claim notice period is short and one-sided.

## Expected review focus

- Realistic claim notice period.
- Reciprocity of time-bar rules.
- Evidence collection period.
- Owner response deadline.
