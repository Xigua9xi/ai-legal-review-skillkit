# Synthetic Sales Contract - Payment Risk

This is a synthetic fixture for testing. It does not describe a real transaction.

## Payment trigger

The buyer shall pay after the end customer completes final project acceptance.

## Invoice

The seller shall issue a valid invoice before payment.

## Risk pattern

The payment obligation depends on a third-party acceptance event that is outside the seller's control. The contract does not provide a fallback payment date, buyer assistance obligation, or late-payment responsibility.

## Expected review focus

- Back-to-back payment risk.
- Missing objective payment deadline.
- Missing overdue payment liability.
- Need for finance and business confirmation.
