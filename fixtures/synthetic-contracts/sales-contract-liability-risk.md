# Synthetic Sales Contract - Liability Risk

This is a synthetic fixture for testing. It does not describe a real transaction.

## Liability

If the seller breaches any obligation, the seller shall compensate the buyer for all losses, including indirect losses, expected profits, attorney fees, investigation fees, and all other expenses.

## Buyer responsibility

The contract does not state liability for buyer delayed payment, delayed acceptance, or failure to provide required information.

## Risk pattern

The seller's liability is broad and uncapped, while buyer-side breach responsibility is missing.

## Expected review focus

- Unlimited liability.
- Indirect loss exposure.
- Lack of reciprocal liability.
- Need for legal negotiation position.
