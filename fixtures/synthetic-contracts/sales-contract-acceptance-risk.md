# Synthetic Sales Contract - Acceptance Risk

This is a synthetic fixture for testing. It does not describe a real transaction.

## Delivery and acceptance

The seller shall deliver goods according to the buyer's requirement. The buyer shall organize acceptance after receiving the goods.

## Quality

The goods shall meet the buyer's use requirements.

## Risk pattern

The acceptance standard is not objective. The buyer's use requirements are not attached or defined. The contract does not specify acceptance documents, objection procedure, or deemed acceptance conditions.

## Expected review focus

- Undefined acceptance standard.
- Missing objection period.
- Missing evidence of acceptance.
- Technical confirmation required.
