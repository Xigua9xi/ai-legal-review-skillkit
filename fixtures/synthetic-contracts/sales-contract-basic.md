# Synthetic Sales Contract - Basic

This is a synthetic fixture for testing. It does not describe a real transaction.

## Parties

Seller: Example Seller Co., Ltd.

Buyer: Example Buyer Co., Ltd.

## Goods

The seller agrees to sell industrial equipment listed in Attachment 1. Attachment 1 is referenced but not included in this fixture.

## Price

The total contract price is RMB 1,000,000, including tax.

## Payment

The buyer shall pay 30% within 10 working days after contract signing and 70% after acceptance.

## Delivery

The seller shall deliver the goods to the buyer's designated warehouse.

## Acceptance

The buyer shall inspect the goods after delivery. If the buyer does not raise an objection within 3 days, the goods are deemed accepted.

## Breach

The seller shall pay liquidated damages for delayed delivery. The contract does not specify buyer late-payment liability.
