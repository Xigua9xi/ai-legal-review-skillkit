# EPC and Engineering Contract Review Rules

Apply these rules only as an extension to the general rules when EPC, construction, engineering, commissioning, project acceptance, or milestone-payment issues are present.

## Project party and funding context

- Check whether the contracting party has a clear role in the project.
- Flag project-company or special-purpose-vehicle structures when funding, guarantee, or payment support is unclear.

## Scope boundary

- Check whether design, procurement, construction, commissioning, grid connection, permits, testing, and documentation responsibilities are allocated clearly.
- Flag conflicts between main contract, technical agreement, quotation sheet, tender documents, and attachments.

## Schedule and delay

- Check start date, completion date, milestone definitions, delay causes, extension rights, and delay damages.
- Flag compressed schedules without extension mechanisms for owner-side delay, force majeure, permit delay, or change orders.

## Price adjustment and change orders

- Check fixed-price risk allocation, material price fluctuation, design changes, owner instructions, and signed variation procedures.
- Flag change procedures that require performance before price/time adjustment is confirmed.

## Quality and performance guarantees

- Check technical standards, test method, commissioning criteria, performance guarantee, warranty start date, and defect liability period.
- Flag vague standards such as "excellent quality" without objective criteria.

## Subcontracting and labor compliance

- Check subcontracting permission, subcontractor qualification, wage payment procedures, safety responsibilities, and compliance documentation.
- Flag responsibility transfer clauses that make one party bear risks outside its control.

## Claims and dispute handling

- Check claim notice periods, evidence requirements, response periods, and whether time bars are balanced.
- Flag unclear arbitration institutions or forum clauses that may be unenforceable.
