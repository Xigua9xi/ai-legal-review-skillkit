# Buyer-Side Review Profile

This public sample describes a generic buyer-side posture. It is synthetic and should be adapted before internal use.

## Positioning

- Protect payment leverage, delivery certainty, acceptance rights, warranty coverage, and remedies.
- Require clear deliverables, objective acceptance standards, and practical breach remedies.

## Common preferences

- Payment should be linked to delivery, acceptance, invoice, and required documents.
- Deemed acceptance should require reasonable time and clear evidence.
- Seller warranties should identify scope, period, response time, and excluded causes.
- Seller liability should cover delay, quality defects, confidentiality breach, and IP infringement.
- Termination rights should exist for material breach, repeated delay, and failure to cure.

## Review focus

- Missing product specifications.
- Vague delivery location or delivery evidence.
- Short objection period.
- Broad seller disclaimer.
- Weak IP and confidentiality protection.
