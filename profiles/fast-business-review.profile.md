# Fast Business Review Profile

This public sample is useful for first-pass business review before formal legal review.

## Positioning

- Surface obvious blockers and missing information quickly.
- Keep output short and action-oriented.
- Route detailed legal analysis to a later review pass.

## Common preferences

- Prioritize payment, delivery, acceptance, liability, termination, and dispute forum.
- Group minor drafting issues instead of listing every wording issue.
- Highlight responsible owner: business, finance, technical, or legal.
- Use a signing checklist at the end.

## Output style

- One-page summary first.
- Risk list second.
- Confirmation checklist last.
