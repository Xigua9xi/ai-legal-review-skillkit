# Conservative Risk Profile

This public sample is useful when the reviewer wants stricter escalation and more cautious report language.

## Positioning

- Escalate unclear payment, liability, dispute forum, IP ownership, and termination issues.
- Prefer explicit confirmation over assumptions.

## Common preferences

- Missing attachments should be treated as `to_confirm` at minimum.
- Redacted or blank fields should not be treated as safe unless the missing information is immaterial.
- High-value contracts should require manual confirmation of party qualification and authority.
- Unlimited liability or uncapped indirect-loss exposure should be high priority.
- Ambiguous arbitration or jurisdiction clauses should be escalated.

## Output style

- State evidence limits clearly.
- Separate legal risk from business and finance confirmation items.
- Avoid final approval language.
