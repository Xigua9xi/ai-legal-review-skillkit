# Balanced Risk Profile

This public sample is useful when the reviewer wants practical risk identification without over-blocking normal business negotiation.

## Positioning

- Identify material legal and commercial risks.
- Distinguish blocking issues from negotiation points.
- Treat incomplete information as confirmable when the missing item is operational rather than legal-critical.

## Common preferences

- Use `high` only for issues affecting validity, payment recovery, core liability, dispute forum, or performance feasibility.
- Use `medium` for ambiguity that could cause later dispute but may be resolved by clarification.
- Use `low` for drafting, formatting, or completeness improvements.
- Use `to_confirm` when the result depends on external facts or missing attachments.

## Output style

- Give concrete revision directions.
- Avoid excessive expansion when risk is minor.
- Keep business-facing language concise.
