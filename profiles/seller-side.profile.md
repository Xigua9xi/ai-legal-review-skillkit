# Seller-Side Review Profile

This public sample describes a generic seller-side posture. It is synthetic and should be adapted before internal use.

## Positioning

- Protect payment recovery, delivery feasibility, liability boundaries, acceptance closure, and evidence retention.
- Avoid open-ended obligations that depend on buyer-side actions outside the seller's control.

## Common preferences

- Payment milestones should be objective and not depend on indefinite buyer confirmation.
- Acceptance standards should be specific, documented, and time-limited.
- Late-payment responsibility should be included.
- Liability should exclude indirect losses where appropriate and include a reasonable cap.
- Change requests should require written confirmation of price and schedule impact.

## Review focus

- Back-to-back payment clauses.
- Buyer unilateral acceptance delay.
- Unlimited liability.
- Excessive delay penalties.
- Broad termination rights without cure periods.
